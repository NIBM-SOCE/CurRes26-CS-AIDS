Feeder and Consumer Map - Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence and Data Science

A shared SLQF Level 3 (Diploma) and Level 4 (HND) stage that progresses to one of two validated Coventry University degrees: Computer Science with Data Science, or Computer Science with Applied Artificial Intelligence. The two degrees share a common core and diverge into variant-specific modules. Every module is placed by what prepares it and what it prepares.

Progression (two routes): BSc (Hons) CS with Data Science  |  BSc (Hons) CS with Applied Artificial Intelligence (NIBU030) Validation partner: Coventry University Architecture: Module records v2.2, issued 13 August 2026 Credit basis: SLQF - 1 credit = 50 notional hours (Industrial Training = 100) Working language: Python (pathway instantiation of the cluster commons) Diploma exit: 41 credits / 13 modules  ·  HND exit: 43 credits / 15 modules Status: Governance submission

The Diploma and HND exit awards each add a separate 4-credit Exit Block taken only by exiting students (Diploma exit 41 credits / 13 modules; HND exit 43 credits / 15 modules). CREST is a mandatory non-credit component and is not counted in the Year 2 credit total. A student progresses to one degree; the Data Science and Applied AI variant modules are alternatives, both prepared by the shared HND. Digital Electronics and Logic Design is carried as a common for cohort formation and has no Coventry feeder in this pathway.
Shared modules. The institution-wide common modules, the cluster commons, the Integrative Skills modules and the exit-block modules are delivered to more than one pathway and their specifications are held in common. No single pathway may vary a shared module's scope, learning outcomes or assessment; changes require the agreement of every pathway that delivers it.
FHEQ (comparability, reference only). The FHEQ column is carried for comparability and reference only. FHEQ is a holistic framework operating at programme and award level; it is not applied module by module to the NIBM-delivered years, where SLQF is the binding framework. "Higher National Diploma" is the award-certificate term and reflects TVEC-side vocabulary; SLQF itself uses "Higher Diploma" for the same level. UK usage of "HND" denotes a qualification of a defined size and character under FHEQ, and no equivalence to it is asserted here: the column states a comparison, not a claim.
